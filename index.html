Saudi Arabia · ZATCA · MPDA · GPCICT · 2022–2026

Enterprise GIS & LOD 500 Delivery

Two national programs requiring engineering-grade data, enterprise governance and coordinated QA/QC.
Challenge

High-precision geospatial deliverables, a governed enterprise SDE backbone and strict QA/QC against ISO/OGC standards — delivered simultaneously.

What I did

Led a cross-functional team, architected SDE geodatabase governance, defined LOD 500 production workflows and built an ArcPy + AI automation pipeline.

Outcome

A centralized governed data environment, 15+ engineering-grade LOD 500 models, 85% reduction in processing time and 98% first-pass QA acceptance.

Key takeaway: Translating field-survey data into governed, engineering-trusted enterprise GIS at national scale — on time and within the ToR.

Enterprise SDE geodatabase governance and lifecycle control.

02

Utility, underground and aerial survey integration.

03

LOD 500 workflows and engineering data acceptance.

ArcPy and AI-assisted automation for QA, migration and reporting.

Sudan · Nigeria · Ethiopia · KETS / Dangote Sugar · 2014–2021

Geospatial Engineering for Agricultural Megaprojects

Sugar, ethanol and water-management programs across large and complex project areas.
Scope

10+ major agricultural infrastructure projects, including terrain, watershed, irrigation and infrastructure analysis across areas exceeding 200,000 hectares.

Technical integration

Integrated Sentinel-2, SRTM, 2 pt/m² LiDAR and RTK-GNSS surveys into unified decision-support GIS systems. LiDAR was validated against 47 RTK control points, achieving 3.2 cm vertical RMSE.

Automation and research

Python terrain automation reduced computation from three days to four hours. Dam-site selection methodology was published in peer-reviewed international journals.

Programs: White Nile Sugar · Savannah Sugar / Dangote · Soba Valley Dam Research · Sugar and Ethanol Programs
