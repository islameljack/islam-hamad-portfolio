06 / METHOD

How I work

A disciplined lifecycle that keeps technical choices connected to the decision the data must support.

Understand

Clarify the requirement, users, constraints and intended decision.

Model

Design the spatial data model, schema and integration approach.

Acquire

Capture, integrate and prepare field, survey, imagery and engineering data.

Govern

Validate metadata, topology, domains, quality and service readiness.

Analyze

Apply spatial analysis, remote sensing or hydrologic modelling.

Automate

Visualize

Turn results into maps, dashboards, web GIS and technical evidence.

Deliver

Provide decision intelligence that can be maintained and trusted.
